BEAST TAG GENERATOR

Two colors in, a pixel-perfect 40×8 rank tag out. Every pixel is computed mathematically from a gradient background, a tinted letterform, and a derived shadow — nothing is hardcoded.

How the colors are used
  • The background is a smooth gradient from Color 1 (left) to Color 2 (right), with a slight top highlight, bottom shading, and darkened edges.
  • The letters stay mostly white but pick up a subtle tint mixed from both colors.
  • The shadow is derived from the letter color — darker and less saturated — never a fixed gray.

Reads beasttagbase.png (40×8px) if present next to these files; otherwise falls back to a built-in template. Rendered entirely on-device — nothing is uploaded anywhere.
